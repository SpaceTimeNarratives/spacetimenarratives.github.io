On Thursday 25th June 2026, the project team met with representatives of the Lake District Holocaust Project and the National Trust to discuss project outcomes and plans for future work. The meeting took place at [Allan Bank in Grasmere in the Lake District](https://www.nationaltrust.org.uk/visit/lake-district/allan-bank-and-grasmere) a Georgian villa in the Lake District, once home to William Wordsworth and National Trust co-founder Canon Rawnsley. We presented our work on the [Corpus of Lake District Writing](https://github.com/UCREL/LakeDistrictCorpus), the transcriptions from the [Lake District Holocaust Project](https://ldhp.org.uk/) and the Holocaust survivor testimonies.
